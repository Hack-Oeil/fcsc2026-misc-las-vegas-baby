Challenge #644

Las Vegas Baby

You are in Las Vegas, in the middle of a Texas Hold'em poker tournament. All the players have gone on a break, and you find yourself alone with the deck of cards and your conscience. You get the urge to cheat.

However, the task is not that easy: the dealer cuts the deck at a random position before dealing the cards. You must be clever so that the deal gives you the best hand, regardless of the cut made by the dealer. In addition, you know you will be in the small blind, but don't yet know if there will be 3 or 4 players after the break.

Your goal is to sort the deck of cards in such a way that you are certain to win the next hand.
